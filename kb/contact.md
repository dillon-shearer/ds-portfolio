# How to contact Dillon

## Contact form

The Contact page (/contact) has a form with name, email and message fields. Messages go straight to Dillon's inbox.

## Email from the chatbot

The chatbot can draft an email to Dillon. It shows a form card with name, email and message fields that the visitor can edit. Nothing is sent until the visitor presses Send, and Cancel discards it.

## Email address

Dillon's site email is dillon@datawithdillon.com.

## Elsewhere

- LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/dillonshearer
- GitHub: github.com/dillon-shearer
- YouTube: youtube.com/@DataWithDillon

The Contact page lists the email, LinkedIn and GitHub links. The YouTube channel is linked from the Videos page (/videos).

## Good reasons to get in touch

- Questions about a project or demo on the site.
- Projects or collaborations in healthcare data, data engineering, analytics or AI tooling.
- Questions about a video or the tools used in it.
