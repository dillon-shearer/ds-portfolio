# Frequently asked questions

## Who is Dillon Shearer?

Dillon works across the data lifecycle, mostly in healthcare and life sciences: data science, data engineering, analytics and AI tooling. See the About page (/about).

## Where does he work?

He is a data scientist at Answer ALS, since February 2022, building ETL pipelines, analytics systems and AI tooling for ALS research. See the work history on the Resume page (/resume).

## Where is he based?

Newnan, Georgia, in the United States. He works remotely.

## What tools does he use?

Mostly Python, SQL and data analysis, with PostgreSQL, Snowflake and Azure, plus Next.js and TypeScript for web projects. He builds with AI coding agents such as Claude Code and Codex. He works with healthcare data standards such as OMOP, SNOMED CT, LOINC and RxNorm. See the skills and tools entry and the Resume page (/resume).

## What kind of work does he do?

ETL pipelines, mapping clinical data to standards, analytics and dashboards, and AI tools built on large language models. His demos show examples: the Gym Tracker and the Variant Report Tool (/demos).

## Can I talk to him about a project?

Questions about projects or collaborations go through the Contact page (/contact). The chatbot does not discuss pay.

## What does he do outside of work?

He works out, rides bicycles and does a lot of athletic things. He used to golf but does less of it now. He is a homebody and has cats. See the interests entry.

## What did he study?

A Bachelor of Business Administration in Management of Information Systems at the University of West Georgia, 2018 to 2022.

## Does he have certifications?

Protecting Human Research Participants (PHRP), April 2025.

## Where can I see his resume?

On the Resume page (/resume), with a PDF download. The chatbot can show it as a card.

## How do I contact him?

Use the Contact page form (/contact), email dillon@datawithdillon.com, or ask the chatbot to draft an email. He is also on LinkedIn and GitHub.

## Does he make videos?

Yes. The Data With Dillon YouTube channel has recorded build sessions with AI agents and data tools. See the Videos page (/videos).

## Is the code for this site public?

Yes, at github.com/dillon-shearer/ds-portfolio. The Site notes page (/colophon) explains the stack.

## Is the gym data real?

Yes. The Gym Tracker (/demos/gym) shows Dillon's own logged workouts. The chatbot can answer questions about the data with its gym data tool.

## Who built the chatbot?

Dillon built it for this site. It runs on AWS or Azure, picked by the visitor, and answers from a small knowledge base about him and the site.
