# About Dillon Shearer

Dillon Shearer works across the data lifecycle, mostly in healthcare and life sciences. His work covers data science, data engineering, data analytics and AI tooling.

## Background

Dillon graduated from the University of West Georgia (UWG) with a Bachelor of Business Administration in Management of Information Systems (MIS). During school he interned as a QA and business analyst at a rare disease data platform. That internship is what got him into healthcare data.

## Current work

Dillon is a data scientist at Answer ALS, where he builds production ETL pipelines, analytics systems and AI tooling for ALS research. The work changes week to week. He has mapped clinical data to the OMOP Common Data Model, built executive dashboards and shipped internal AI tools, usually whatever the research effort needs next.

## Location

Dillon lives in Newnan, Georgia, in the United States, and works remotely.

## Outside of work

Outside of work Dillon is usually building something anyway. This site and the Gym Tracker dashboard on it are the current examples. He also records build sessions for the Data With Dillon YouTube channel. See the interests entry for his hobbies.
