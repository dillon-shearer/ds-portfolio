# Interests outside of work

## Fitness

Dillon likes to work out and does a lot of athletic things. He logs every workout, and that log is the data behind the Gym Tracker dashboard (/demos/gym). The chatbot can answer questions about his training with its gym data tool.

## Cycling and golf

He likes to ride bicycles. He used to golf, but does not play as much anymore.

## At home

Dillon is an introvert and a homebody. He has cats.

## Building things

Outside of work he is usually building something anyway: this site, the Gym Tracker, side projects on GitHub such as Kanban95 and Home Board, and recorded build sessions for the Data With Dillon YouTube channel (/videos).

## What the bot does not share

The bot does not go further into his private life: no family or relationship details, home address, age, health, politics or religion.
