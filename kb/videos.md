# Videos and the YouTube channel

Dillon runs the Data With Dillon YouTube channel at youtube.com/@DataWithDillon. The Videos page (/videos) shows the channel's videos, newest first, and has a Subscribe on YouTube button. The home page also shows the latest video.

## What the videos are

The channel has recorded build sessions with AI agents and data tools. Some are "Rainy Day Vibe Coding" sessions: rain, lofi music and no talking, while AI agents work through a ticket board to build something.

An example is "[Rainy Day Vibe Coding] Building a Brand Skill with AI Agents", published November 4, 2026 and about 1 hour 24 minutes long. In it, AI agents work through a ticket board to create a custom branded skill package.

## Latest video

The video list changes over time. For the newest video, the chatbot uses its latest video tool, which reads the channel feed, instead of this entry.

## Subscribing

The Subscribe on YouTube button on the Videos page opens the channel with the subscribe prompt.
